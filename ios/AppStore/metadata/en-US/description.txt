Podrida Score keeps the score for Podrida, the trick-taking card game where everyone calls how many hands they'll win each turn.

It's a ledger, like the one you'd draw on paper: every player gets a column, every turn gets a line. Enter what each player called and what they won, and the score adds itself up.

SCORING
• Exact call: 5 points, plus 3 for each hand won
• Miss: minus 5, and minus 3 for each hand off
• Each line shows what every player scored that turn, and the totals stay pinned at the bottom
• The leader's total gets a high-score stamp

THE RULE, CHECKED
Once everyone has called, the calls in a turn can't add up to the turn number. Podrida Score spots it and waits until someone changes their call. Turns unlock one at a time, so no one scores the wrong line.

MADE FOR THE TABLE
• Up to 24 players and 300 turns
• Player names and totals stay in view while you scroll
• Rename players or add one mid-game
• Calls first, then results: the keyboard moves through a turn the way it's played
• The screen stays on while you play
• Your game is saved as you go, even if you close the app

Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.
