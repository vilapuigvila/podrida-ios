# Podrida Score — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 24 September 2026*

Podrida Score does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- **No account, no sign-in.** The app has no account system.
- **Your game stays on your device.** Player names and scores are saved only on your iPhone or iPad, so a game survives the app being closed. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **No network access.** The app works entirely offline and doesn't connect to any server.
- **No tracking, analytics, advertising or third-party services.**
